# M7.5 Performance pass, 23 November 2026

Budgets are from PLAN.md 9.10. Measured with `web/scripts/perf.mjs` on the production build, before (commit f36f626) and after. Raw results: `m7.5-before.json`, `m7.5-after.json`.

## The test phone

A stand-in, not a phone. A 412 x 915 viewport at 2.6x, the CPU slowed 4x, a 4G link (9 Mbit/s, 100 ms round trip), brotli like Cloudflare Pages. The GPU is the laptop's Intel UHD, the display refreshes at 144 Hz, and the vector map tiles come from OpenFreeMap over the real network. The numbers compare two builds on one machine. They do not replace a run on a real mid-range Android phone.

## Result against the budgets

| Budget | Before | After | |
|---|---|---|---|
| First paint under 1.5 s | 1.43 s | 1.40 s | met, no change |
| Live globe under 3 s | 3.10 s | 2.75 s | met now |
| Initial JS under 350 KB compressed | 88 KB (entry) | 88 KB | met, see below |
| Pixel ratio capped at 2 | capped | capped | already so |
| Pause when the tab is hidden | 144 frames and 6 timers a second | 0 and 0 | met, see below |
| 50 fps while flying | 33 to 38 fps | 32 to 39 fps | not met on this proxy |

Medians of three cold loads. First paint runs: 1.32, 1.43, 1.44 s before; 1.37, 1.40, 1.45 s after. Live globe runs: 3.09, 3.10, 3.22 s before; 2.71, 2.75, 3.03 s after.

**JavaScript.** The page's own script is 88 KB brotli (96 KB gzip). The map chunk (MapLibre) is 250 KB brotli (281 KB gzip) and starts downloading at once, so the two together are 338 KB brotli, 377 KB gzip. Read as "everything before the globe", that is under 350 KB with brotli, which is what Pages serves, and over it with gzip. deck.gl is its own set of chunks, 250 KB brotli, and starts downloading once the map chunk has run, beside the map's own tiles.

**Hidden tab.** Counted in a page told it is hidden, apart from deck.gl's own idle loop, which the browser stops in a really hidden tab anyway. Before: the globe turning asked for 144 frames a second hidden and visible alike, and an open facility's flipbook kept 6 timers a second. After: both are 0 while hidden, and back to the visible rates the moment the tab returns.

**Flying.** The globe turning on its own holds 53 to 57 fps in clean runs. Flights to a facility run 32 to 39 fps and the worst second has 15 to 19 frames. Four runs, before and after taken alternately so machine noise falls on both:

| | turn | flight near | flight far |
|---|---|---|---|
| before | 54.3, 39.9 | 37.9, 37.6 | 33.1, 32.7 |
| after | 56.5, 55.1 | 36.6, 38.8 | 32.5, 31.5 |

The 39.9 is a noisy run, not a trend. Single runs on this laptop swing by 10 fps.

## What changed

- The map's code is asked for as soon as the page's own script runs, on the routes that have a globe, and the tile server's connection is opened with it (`index.html`, `vite.config.ts`). Before, the map was only asked for once the app had started. This is the 340 ms.
- A hidden tab stops the globe's slow turn and picks it up again on return, stops the flipbook clock and the month player, and pauses every CSS animation (`app/visibility.ts` and the places that use it).
- The deck.gl layers are no longer handed to deck.gl again on every zoom frame when the two shares the zoom controls have not changed.

## What was tried and did not help

Each was built as a throwaway variant and measured on the same phone. Differences under about 4 fps are noise here.

- **No halo and bloom layers.** No gain in flights (33 and 27 fps). PLAN.md 9.10 says to drop the halo first if 50 fps is missed. On this evidence the halo is not what costs.
- **No backdrop blur on the panel.** 35 and 34 fps against 34 and 32.
- **No label layers.** 40 and 27 fps. Noise.
- **Pixel ratio 1.** 39 and 38 fps against 34 and 32. The only change with a steady effect, and not enough alone. It would also break the cap of 2 in 9.10, which is a ceiling and not a floor.

A profile of a flight is flat. Of the main thread's time in a 4.5 s flight, MapLibre took 37 percent (mostly per-tile matrix and uniform work), deck.gl 23 percent (mostly GL state tracking and container bounds) and the browser's own work 18 percent. No single function was above 3 percent, so there is nothing to fix in one place.

## Not done

- A run on a real mid-range Android phone, which is what M7.5 asks for. Until then the flight rate is a laptop proxy.
- deck.gl's loop keeps asking for a frame at the display rate while the page is open and nothing is drawing. Stopping it means reaching into the overlay's private `_deck` and restarting luma's loop afterwards, which was not tested, so it was left alone. The browser stops it in a hidden tab.
- If a real phone also misses 50 fps in flights, the candidates are a lower pixel ratio while the camera moves (about 4 fps here) and fewer map layers close in.
