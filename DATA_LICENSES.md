# Data licences and attribution

The MIT licence in this repo covers the code only. Each data source keeps its own terms. This list follows PLAN.md section 7.3 and grows as sources are added.

| Source | Used for | Terms and attribution | In use since |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenFreeMap vector tiles | Basemap | Free public tiles. Show the OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles and "OpenStreetMap contributors" attribution on the map. | M0 |
| OpenStreetMap | Basemap data | ODbL. Attribution as above. | M0 |
| Copernicus Sentinel-5P | NO2 columns | Free and open. Credit "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data [year]". | Planned, M1 |
| ERA5 | Wind | Credit the Copernicus Climate Change Service. | Planned, M3 |
| EEA Industrial Reporting database | Reported NOx, EU | Reuse with attribution. Exact wording still to verify on the dataset page. | Planned, M1 |
| EPA CAMPD | Validation, US | US government data, credit EPA. | Planned, M4 |
| Beirle et al. NOx point source catalogue v2 | Early demo | CC BY 4.0. Cite the paper. | Planned, M2 |
